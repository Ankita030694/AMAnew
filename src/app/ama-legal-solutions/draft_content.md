## Priority 3: Legal Notice and Drafting: Precision is Key

A legal notice is not just a letter; it is a formal legal document that can determine the outcome of a future court case.

### Drafting with Strategic Foresight
Every word in a legal notice matters. We draft notices for:
-   **Section 138 (Cheque Bounce)**: Mandatory 15-day notice to initiate criminal proceedings.
-   **Money Recovery**: Formal demand for unpaid dues with a specific timeline.
-   **Contractual Breaches**: Highlighting violations of agreements and seeking remedies.
-   **Property Disputes**: Asserting rights over real estate assets.

### The Importance of Physical Delivery
While emails are convenient, physical post with an Acknowledgment Due (AD) card is still the gold standard for evidence in Indian courts. We ensure all our notices follow this process to provide indisputable proof of service.
